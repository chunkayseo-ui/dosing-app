Pool operations · field card

Chemical dosing routes

One card per pool, one lap per pool. Stand at the red START marker, face the direction of the first arrow, and keep a steady pace so the dose spreads evenly. Stop at the END flag.

How to read the card

StartFilled red dot. Always begin here.
Walk directionChevrons repeat along the whole route.
EndBlack flag. Route is complete.
Red lineWalk it once, dosing as you go.
Deck and waterOrange is deck you walk on, blue is water.
LadderPool entry ladder. Do not pour over it.
Black barPool divider between sections.
Dark blockPillar standing in the pool.
WS circleWhere to collect the water sample for testing.
Green lineHigh wall. You cannot see or pour over it.
Red hatchingDeck too narrow to walk. Route skips it.
Pale bandShallow ledge. Pour past its edge.
Dashed greyWalking only — stairs, or between pools. No dosing.
