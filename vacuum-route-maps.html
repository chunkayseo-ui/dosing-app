Pool operations · vacuum card

Vacuum Route

One card per pool. Connect the hose at the port marked VP or SP, begin at the red START marker, and work the vacuum head along the route in the direction of the arrows. Stop at the END flag.

Choose a pool

How to read the card
StartFilled red dot. Always begin here.
Walk directionChevrons repeat along the whole route.
EndBlack flag. Route is complete.
Red lineWork it once, vacuuming as you go.
Deck and waterOrange is deck you walk on, blue is water.
LadderPool entry ladder. Work around its feet.
Black barPool divider between sections.
Dark blockPillar standing in the pool.
VP squareVacuum port. Connect the hose here.
SP squareSkimmer port. Connect the hose here.
Yellow strokesVacuum head pattern. Overlap each pass, follow the numbers.
Photo pointStand at the badge and point the camera the way its arrow goes, along the coloured band. The time must show on every photo.
Green lineHigh wall. You cannot reach past it.
Red hatchingDeck too narrow to walk. Route skips it.
Pale bandShallow ledge. Pour past its edge.

HS — Two pools

Same size · 1 lap each · pool 1 first

Tap to enlarge

Route

Pools
Two, same size. Pool 2 sits turned 90°
Order
Pool 1 first, then pool 2
Pool 1
Start on the top deck above the vacuum port. Left along the top and down the left side, walk back, then down the right side. Bottom is skipped
Pool 2
Walk across to the vacuum port. Down the right side and along the bottom, then walk back to the port. Left and top are skipped
Laps
1 per pool
Vacuum pattern
Pool 1: 1 middle lane from the top deck, 2 left lane from the left deck, 3 right lane from the right deck. Pool 2: 4 the whole pool from the right deck on the way down, 5 bottom ledge
Watch for
Ladder, 2 dividers and 2 shallow ledges in each pool
Small deck
Pool 2 left side, at the ladder — not on the route
Vacuum ports
Pool 1: top right, just left of the ledge. Pool 2: top right, below the ledge — connect the hose at each pool’s own port

Red hatched decks are too narrow to walk — do not step on them. Finish pool 1 before moving. Nothing is vacuumed on the walk across to pool 2.
